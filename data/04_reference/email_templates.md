# Order desk email templates

Copy, paste, fill in the bits in [brackets]. Sign off with your own name.

---

## 1. Order acknowledgement

> **Subject:** Order received: [their PO / ref]
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> Thanks for your order [their PO / ref]. It's on our system as order [Orbit order no.] and we're aiming to deliver on [delivery date].
>
> If anything on it is out of stock we'll let you know straight away.
>
> Kind regards,
> [name]
> Meridian Catering Supplies | 01480 220 400

## 2. Delivery update ("where's my order")

> **Subject:** Re: [their subject]
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> Your order [Orbit order no. / their ref] is [status in plain English] and is due with you on [delivery date].
>
> [Anything else they need to know.]
>
> Kind regards,
> [name]

## 3. Account on hold (send only after checking with Tom)

> **Subject:** Re: [their subject]
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> Thanks for your order. Unfortunately we can't process it at the moment as there's a hold on the account. Please contact Tom Reilly in our accounts team on 01480 220 404 or accounts@meridiancatering.co.uk and we'll get it moving as soon as that's sorted.
>
> Kind regards,
> [name]

## 4. Out of stock / substitution

> **Subject:** Re: [their subject]
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> [Item] on your order [ref] is out of stock until [date]. [We can offer [alternative] at [price] instead / We'll send the rest now and that item when it arrives.] Let us know which you'd prefer.
>
> Kind regards,
> [name]

## 5. Price query

> **Subject:** Re: [their subject]
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> Thanks for your order [ref]. The price on your PO for [item] is [their price]; our current price for your account is [our price]. [Reason, e.g. new price list from 1 July.] Can you confirm you're happy to go ahead at [our price]?
>
> Kind regards,
> [name]
